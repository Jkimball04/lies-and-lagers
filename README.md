# Lies & Lagers

A host-run bluffing party game for a group in one room: everyone gets a strange word, an odd question or a picture, writes a made-up answer that sounds true, and votes for the one they think is real.

- **Host:** open `host.html` on a laptop, then press **Open TV screen** and put that window on the TV.
- **Players:** scan the QR code on the TV with a phone camera and type your name.

Prompts and pictures come from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons; each picture's credit and licence is shown at the reveal.
